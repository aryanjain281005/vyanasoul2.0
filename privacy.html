Privacy notice

Last updated 22 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to the details you share with Vyana Soul (Rekha Bala, Mumbai) when you request a session on this website.

What we collect

When you send a session request, we receive what you type in the form: your name, your WhatsApp number or email, the session you chose, where you live, your preferred day, time and language, and anything you write about what you'd like to explore. Your browser also tells us your time zone, so Rekha can offer times that suit you.

To stop automated spam, we keep a scrambled code made from your internet address. It can't be turned back into the address.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange your session and keep a record of it. We don't sell your details, share them for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Who sees it

Rekha, and the developer who looks after this website when something needs fixing. Your request is stored with Supabase, our database provider, and a copy of it is sent to Rekha's phone through Telegram. If you give an email address, a confirmation is sent through our email provider.

How long we keep it

For 24 months after your last session. After that it is deleted, or kept only without anything that identifies you.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time. Write to vyanasoul369@gmail.com or message +91 99874 87242 on WhatsApp, and we'll do it within 30 days.

Cookies

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. While you fill in the booking form, your browser keeps a draft in this tab only, so a reload doesn't lose it. The draft is removed once your request is sent or the tab is closed.

Spiritual guidance

Readings and healing sessions offer spiritual guidance. They are not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal or financial advice.
