Questions

What people usually ask before their first session.

What is an Akashic Records reading?

The Akashic Records are understood as a record of every soul's journey across its lifetimes. Through a specific prayer, a trained reader opens your record and receives guidance about your purpose, karmic patterns, gifts and the lessons you are working through in this life. It is guidance, not fortune-telling: take what resonates and leave what doesn't.

How do online sessions work?

Sessions take place on Zoom or Google Meet. After booking you receive a confirmation with the meeting link and a short preparation guide. In the session you settle in and set your intention, Rekha opens your record, and you ask your questions. With your permission the session is recorded for you.

What should I expect from my first session?

A short introduction, then intention setting while Rekha opens your record. Expect insight into your soul's journey, your current life themes and answers to your questions. Writing your questions down beforehand helps.

Which languages do you offer?

English and Hindi.

Can I meet in person?

Sessions are mostly online so they're open to anyone, anywhere. If you're in Mumbai, an in-person session can be arranged on request.

How do I pay?

Payment details are shared when Rekha confirms your session. Courses above ₹5,000 can be paid in two or three instalments.
